Growthify for developers

Your store's Growthify data can be read and changed from outside Growthify, by apps you connect yourself. Your own code with an access key, an assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT, a server that hears about each change through a webhook, or a calendar that shows when campaigns send. You set them all up on the web dashboard under Other apps, one store at a time, on every plan, Free included.

This section is for merchants who want a nightly review digest or a campaign report in their own sheet, the developer or agency they hire, and anyone wiring Growthify into Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code or Cursor. It covers what each way in can reach, what it can never reach, and the exact requests and messages involved.

Four ways in​

WayWhat it's forRead
Access key and the APIYour code lists this week's reviews, pauses a popup, or moves a bundle's end dateAccess keys, The API
An assistantAsk Claude or ChatGPT "which popups are on right now?" or "reply to yesterday's one-star reviews"Assistants
WebhooksGrowthify sends a signed message to your server when a review comes in or a COD order is confirmedWebhooks
Calendar feedGoogle Calendar or Apple Calendar shows when campaigns send and sales start and endCalendar feeds

Making, showing or replacing a key or a webhook secret asks you to have signed in within the last 10 minutes. If two-step sign-in is on, it asks for the six digits too.

One store at a time​

Every key, assistant connection, webhook address and calendar feed is made for one of the stores linked to your Growthify account. It never reaches another store, even one on the same account. An agency with three client stores makes three keys. When an assistant connects, the consent page asks which store it reaches; connect it again for a second store.

The store also decides the limits, because plans in Growthify belong to a store and Shopify bills them.

What an outside app can reach​

Growthify holds two kinds of data, and they are treated differently.

Your store data: read and change. Only what each key or assistant is ticked for.

PartWhat's in it
Store settingsOrder and stock notices, the weekly summary, the widget colour, the credit line
ModulesWhich of the 16 modules are on, and what the store's plan includes
CampaignsPopups, email and SMS campaigns, countdowns and announcement bars
Bundles and offersBundles, quantity breaks, buy one get one, cart upsells
RulesDiscount stacking, price markups, product sync rules
Review moderationApprove or hide a review, feature it, and your replies
ReportsReport definitions, schedules and finished runs

Delete is its own tick, off unless you choose it, and only on parts where a row can go. An assistant never deletes on its own: it asks you first.

Your shoppers' records: read only. Never changed or deleted from outside Growthify, whatever a key says. Only the fields listed go out.

RecordsWhat goes outWhat never does
Email and SMS subscribersEmail or phone number, first and last name, status, where they signed up, signed up on, tagsThe messages you sent them
Chat conversationsVisitor name, status, rating, each message, who wrote it and whenThe visitor's email or browser id
Loyalty membersEmail, points, lifetime points, tier, joined onTheir order history
Cash on delivery ordersOrder name, total, verification status, verified or cancelled onThe customer's email or phone: those stay in Shopify
Saved addressesName, company, address lines, city, province, country and postcodeThe phone number
WishlistsHow many shoppers saved each product, when it was last savedWho saved it
Review authorsThe name they gave, verified purchase or notTheir email or order id

What no outside app can ever do​

  • Change, delete or message a shopper. Sending a campaign stays a button you press in Growthify.
  • Reach another store, even one on this same account.
  • See your sign-in, two-step codes, keys, webhook secrets or Shopify billing.
  • Change the store's plan, uninstall Growthify or link a new store.
  • Delete anything an assistant asks to delete, until you say yes.
  • Read a module's own configuration (it can hold provider passwords and keys), a campaign's sender address or audience, or where a finished report is emailed and its file.

Requests from every key and assistant on a store share the day's number, and the count starts again at midnight UTC. Past it, apps are asked to wait until then; nothing in the store is touched. Other apps shows how much of each the store uses. A store over a lowered limit keeps what it has and can't add more until it's under. Plan prices are on Billing plans.

FAQ​

Where do I set up keys, assistants and webhooks?​

On the web dashboard under Other apps. Everything is made for one connected store at a time. The embedded app in the Shopify admin keeps working as it always has.

Can an outside app change my shoppers' details?​

No. Shopper records are read only from outside Growthify, and only the fields in the table above go out. Webhooks don't carry them at all.

Can an assistant delete things in my store?​

It can only ask. You get a link to Growthify showing the item, and nothing goes until you press Delete it within 10 minutes.

Does my old Merchant API v1 key still work?​

Yes, as it did. Moving from Merchant API v1 covers replacing it.

Do I need a paid plan?​

No. Every plan has keys, assistants, webhooks and calendar feeds; the plans differ in how many. A paid module still checks the store's plan where it runs, so switching one on through the API doesn't buy it.
