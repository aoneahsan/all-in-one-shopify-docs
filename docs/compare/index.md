# Moving to Growthify from another Shopify app

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** One guide for each app below: what it costs, what its listing says it does, and what you'd have to set up again.

## Read this first

- Growthify is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so you can't install it on your store today. You [join a waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact).
- It has no order or shipping confirmation emails. Shopify keeps sending its own.
- There's no standalone discount code manager. Codes get made for popup submissions and review incentives, and nowhere else.

## The guides

- [Vitals](/compare/vitals): A paid all-in-one app that puts reviews, bundles, upsells, popups, wishlists and 40-plus other tools under one subscription priced by monthly orders.
- [Growave](/compare/growave): A retention app that combines loyalty points, referrals, reviews and wishlists, with a free tier up to 200 orders a month.
- [Wooview All in one](/compare/wooview): A smaller one-stop app covering product reviews, wishlists, signup popups, sales popups and email marketing, priced by monthly orders.
- [Judge.me](/compare/judgeme): A product reviews app with an unlimited free plan and one $15 paid plan that adds coupons, referrals and integrations.
- [Loox](/compare/loox): A photo and video reviews app with a free plan up to 500 orders, AI sorting and summaries on paid plans, and discounts offered in exchange for photo reviews.
- [Yotpo Product Reviews](/compare/yotpo-reviews): The reviews product from Yotpo, free up to 50 orders a month, with paid plans whose price changes with order volume and may be billed outside Shopify.
- [Klaviyo](/compare/klaviyo): An email, SMS and WhatsApp marketing platform with signup forms, abandoned-checkout flows and segmentation, free up to 250 profiles and billed by contacts beyond that.
- [Omnisend](/compare/omnisend): An email and SMS marketing app with popups, signup forms and abandoned-cart automations, free up to 250 contacts and priced by list size after that.
- [Smile.io](/compare/smile): A loyalty app with points, VIP tiers and referrals, free up to 200 orders a month and priced by monthly orders above that.
- [Rivo](/compare/rivo): A loyalty, rewards and referrals app with a free points program up to 200 orders and a Scale plan whose price is capped at $499 a month however many orders you take.
- [Swym Wishlist Plus](/compare/swym-wishlist-plus): A wishlist app priced by wishlist actions a month, with price-drop and restock alerts, POS access to wishlists and APIs on the top plan.
- [Upsell.com](/compare/upsell-com-reconvert): An upsell app, formerly ReConvert, covering product, cart, checkout, thank-you page and one-click post-purchase offers, free up to 50 orders and priced by orders or upsell revenue after that.
- [Frequently Bought Together](/compare/frequently-bought-together): A bundle recommendation app that shows one-click add-to-cart bundles on product and cart pages, with four bundle discount types and a free plan of three manual bundles.
- [PageFly](/compare/pagefly): A page builder with AI page generation, A/B tests, heatmaps and a cart drawer in beta, free for one published page or section.

Plan prices and feature rows side by side are on the [comparison pages](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/compare).
