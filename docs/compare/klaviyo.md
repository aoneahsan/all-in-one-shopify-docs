# Moving from Klaviyo to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** Klaviyo is an email, SMS and WhatsApp marketing platform with signup forms, abandoned-checkout flows and segmentation. It's free up to 250 profiles and billed by contacts beyond that. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/klaviyo-email-marketing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Klaviyo's Shopify listing</a> is where these facts came from.

If email and SMS are where most of your sales start, Klaviyo is built for you: predictive segments, send-time optimization and 350-plus integrations sit behind every send. Growthify treats campaigns and abandoned-cart emails as one module beside reviews and bundles. It sends through an email provider you already pay for, and the bill isn't tied to your contact count.

Growthify isn't on the Shopify App Store yet, so nobody can install it today. You can [join the waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact). Until then, a working Klaviyo account is worth keeping.

## The email side is the whole question

1. **Keep your list where it lives.** Your subscribers stay with whoever holds them today. Nothing in Growthify imports Klaviyo segments or flows.
2. **Pick the provider that would do the sending.** Klaviyo sends your email itself; Growthify doesn't. You'd connect a provider and pay that provider's bill.
3. **Write down the flows you'd rebuild.** Abandoned cart and campaigns are covered in [Admin features: marketing and growth](/admin/features#marketing-and-growth) and the [Cart](/modules/cart) module.
4. **Move your signup forms last.** Growthify's version is the [Popups](/modules/popups) module.
5. **Check Shopify's own emails stay on.** Growthify sends no order or shipping confirmations, so Shopify keeps sending those.
6. **Read [Installation](/getting-started/installation)** once installing is possible.

One more limit: there's no standalone discount code manager. Growthify makes codes for popup submissions and review incentives only.
