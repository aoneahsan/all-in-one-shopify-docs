# Moving from Klaviyo to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** An email, SMS and WhatsApp marketing platform with signup forms, abandoned-checkout flows and segmentation, free up to 250 profiles and billed by contacts beyond that. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/klaviyo-email-marketing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Klaviyo's listing yourself</a>.

## You can't move yet

- Growthify is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so you can't install it on your store today. You [join a waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact).
- It has no order or shipping confirmation emails. Shopify keeps sending its own.
- There's no standalone discount code manager. Codes get made for popup submissions and review incentives, and nowhere else.

So if Klaviyo is doing its job, stay on it. This page is here so you know what a move would involve.

## Who each one suits

Choose Klaviyo if email and SMS are your main sales channel and you want predictive segments, send-time optimization and 350-plus integrations behind them.

Choose Growthify if campaigns and abandoned-cart emails are a module beside your reviews and bundles, sent through an email provider you already pay for, rather than a platform billed by contact count.

## What a move would involve

Klaviyo sends your email itself. Growthify doesn't: it sends through a provider you connect, so you'd still have an email provider's bill.

Your subscriber list stays with whoever holds it today. Nothing here imports Klaviyo segments or flows.
