# Moving from Rivo to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** Rivo is a loyalty, rewards and referrals app with a free points program up to 200 orders and a Scale plan whose price is capped at $499 a month however many orders you take. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/rivo-loyalty" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Rivo's listing</a> is the source.

Two things set Rivo apart: a developer toolkit for custom work, and that $499 cap, which you can plan a budget around at high order volumes. Growthify suits a store where loyalty and referrals on its Growth plan are enough, and where it's handier to have them in the same app as popups, reviews and bundles.

Growthify isn't on the Shopify App Store yet. There's nothing to install today, only a [waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact).

## Two things to untangle

**Points balances.** As with any loyalty app, these are the hard part of leaving. Nothing in Growthify imports points balances from Rivo.

**Custom work.** If a developer has built on Rivo's toolkit, that work hangs off Rivo, and nothing in Growthify replaces it. Ask whoever built it what it does before you plan anything.

With both answered, the reading is short: [Admin features: marketing and growth](/admin/features#marketing-and-growth) for the loyalty and referral screens, [Plans and limits](/admin/entitlements) for which plan carries them, and [Installation](/getting-started/installation) for when installing becomes possible.

Growthify also leaves two jobs to others. Order and shipping confirmations stay with Shopify. And there's no standalone discount code manager; codes come only from popup submissions and review incentives.
