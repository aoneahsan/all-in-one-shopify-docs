# Moving from Rivo to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** A loyalty, rewards and referrals app with a free points program up to 200 orders and a Scale plan whose price is capped at $499 a month however many orders you take. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/rivo-loyalty" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Rivo's listing yourself</a>.

## You can't move yet

- Growthify is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so you can't install it on your store today. You [join a waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact).
- It has no order or shipping confirmation emails. Shopify keeps sending its own.
- There's no standalone discount code manager. Codes get made for popup submissions and review incentives, and nowhere else.

So if Rivo is doing its job, stay on it. This page is here so you know what a move would involve.

## Who each one suits

Choose Rivo if you want a loyalty and referral program with a developer toolkit for custom work, and a price cap you can plan around at high order volumes.

Choose Growthify if loyalty and referrals on the Growth plan are enough, and you would rather they sat in the same app as your popups, reviews and bundles.

## What a move would involve

Points balances are the hard part of leaving a loyalty app, and Rivo's developer toolkit may have custom work hanging off it too.

Nothing here imports points balances from Rivo, or replaces custom work built on its toolkit.
