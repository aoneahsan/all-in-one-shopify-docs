# Moving from Swym Wishlist Plus to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** A wishlist app priced by wishlist actions a month, with price-drop and restock alerts, POS access to wishlists and APIs on the top plan. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/swym-relay" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Swym Wishlist Plus's listing yourself</a>.

## You can't move yet

- Growthify is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so you can't install it on your store today. You [join a waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact).
- It has no order or shipping confirmation emails. Shopify keeps sending its own.
- There's no standalone discount code manager. Codes get made for popup submissions and review incentives, and nowhere else.

So if Swym Wishlist Plus is doing its job, stay on it. This page is here so you know what a move would involve.

## Who each one suits

Choose Swym Wishlist Plus if the wishlist is a core part of how your shoppers buy and you want multiple lists, sharing, POS access and a 30-day trial to prove it.

Choose Growthify if a wishlist with 500 items on the free plan and 5,000 on Pro covers you, and you would rather not pay $29.99 a month for that one block.

## What a move would involve

Your shoppers' saved lists live with Swym, so that's the thing to think about before anything else.

Nothing here imports saved wishlists from Swym, and shoppers would start their lists again.
