# Moving from Swym Wishlist Plus to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** Swym Wishlist Plus is a wishlist app priced by wishlist actions a month, with price-drop and restock alerts, POS access to wishlists and APIs on the top plan. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/swym-relay" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Swym's listing</a> is what we checked.

Swym is worth its price when the wishlist is a core part of how your shoppers buy: multiple lists, sharing, POS access, and a 30-day trial to prove it. Growthify's wishlist holds 500 items on the free plan and 5,000 on Pro. If that covers you, the question is whether $29.99 a month for that one block is worth paying.

Growthify can't be installed yet; it isn't on the Shopify App Store. [Join the waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact) and keep Swym until then.

## Your shoppers' lists stay with Swym

Saved wishlists live in Swym, and nothing in Growthify imports them. Shoppers would start their lists again. Think about that before anything else, because it's the one cost your customers would notice.

If you'd accept it:

1. **Compare your list sizes with the item limits** in [Plans and limits](/admin/billing-plans).
2. **Check what you use from Swym's alerts, sharing and POS access.** None of those appear in Growthify's rows on this page.
3. **Read the [Wishlist](/modules/wishlist) module page** for the block itself.
4. **[Install](/getting-started/installation)** when installing becomes possible.

Growthify doesn't send order or shipping confirmations, which stay with Shopify. Nor does it have a standalone discount code manager: codes are made for popup submissions and review incentives only.
