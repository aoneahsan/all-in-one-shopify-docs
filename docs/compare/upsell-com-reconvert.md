# Moving from Upsell.com to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** An upsell app, formerly ReConvert, covering product, cart, checkout, thank-you page and one-click post-purchase offers, free up to 50 orders and priced by orders or upsell revenue after that. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/reconvert-upsell-cross-sell" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Upsell.com's listing yourself</a>.

## You can't move yet

- Growthify is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so you can't install it on your store today. You [join a waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact).
- It has no order or shipping confirmation emails. Shopify keeps sending its own.
- There's no standalone discount code manager. Codes get made for popup submissions and review incentives, and nowhere else.

So if Upsell.com is doing its job, stay on it. This page is here so you know what a move would involve.

## Who each one suits

Choose Upsell.com if post-purchase funnels with A/B tests are the work in front of you, and a plan that grows with your order count from $9.99 suits a small store.

Choose Growthify if you want cart cross-sells, bundles and the thank-you page upsell on one flat plan, not a separate bill stepped by orders.

## What a move would involve

Upsell.com's funnels sit on the thank-you page and after checkout, so compare those screens first.

Nothing here imports Upsell.com funnels. You'd set the offers up again.
