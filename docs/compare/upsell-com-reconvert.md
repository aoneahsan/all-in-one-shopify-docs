# Moving from Upsell.com to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** Upsell.com, formerly ReConvert, is an upsell app covering product, cart, checkout, thank-you page and one-click post-purchase offers. It's free up to 50 orders and priced by orders or upsell revenue after that. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/reconvert-upsell-cross-sell" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Upsell.com's listing</a> is our source.

If post-purchase funnels with A/B tests are the work in front of you, Upsell.com is shaped around them, and a plan that starts at $9.99 and grows with your order count suits a small store. Growthify puts cart cross-sells, bundles and the thank-you page upsell on one flat plan instead of a separate bill stepped by orders.

There's no switching today. Growthify has no App Store listing yet and nothing to install. The [waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact) is open.

## Walk your funnel from the end

Upsell.com's funnels sit on the thank-you page and after checkout, so compare those screens first and work backwards to the cart.

1. **Screenshot each live funnel.** Nothing in Growthify imports Upsell.com funnels; you'd set every offer up again.
2. **Thank-you and post-purchase offers:** [Post-purchase and thank-you upsells](/modules/upsells).
3. **Cart and product-page offers:** [Cart](/modules/cart).
4. **Bundles you've been running:** [Bundles](/modules/bundles).
5. **[Install](/getting-started/installation)** once it's possible.

If an offer hands out a discount code, check it first: Growthify makes codes for popup submissions and review incentives, and has no standalone discount code manager. Order and shipping confirmations aren't its job either; Shopify keeps sending its own.
