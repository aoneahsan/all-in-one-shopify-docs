# Moving from Wooview All in one to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** A smaller one-stop app covering product reviews, wishlists, signup popups, sales popups and email marketing, priced by monthly orders. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/wooview-all-in-one" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Wooview All in one's listing yourself</a>.

## You can't move yet

- Growthify is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so you can't install it on your store today. You [join a waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact).
- It has no order or shipping confirmation emails. Shopify keeps sending its own.
- There's no standalone discount code manager. Codes get made for popup submissions and review incentives, and nowhere else.

So if Wooview All in one is doing its job, stay on it. This page is here so you know what a move would involve.

## Who each one suits

Choose Wooview if reviews, a wishlist and two kinds of popup are all you need, and a $19 plan for up to 500 orders a month is the right size.

Choose Growthify if the same four jobs are a quarter of the list and you also want bundles, upsells, loyalty, consent, reports and a page builder in the same subscription.

## What a move would involve

Wooview's four jobs map onto three Growthify modules and one screen.

Email campaigns in Growthify need your own email provider's key. Wooview's listing doesn't ask for one.
