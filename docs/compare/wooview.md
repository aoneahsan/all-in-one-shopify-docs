# Moving from Wooview All in one to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** Wooview All in one is a smaller one-stop app covering product reviews, wishlists, signup popups, sales popups and email marketing, priced by monthly orders. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/wooview-all-in-one" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Wooview's listing</a> has the rest.

If reviews, a wishlist and two kinds of popup are all your store needs, Wooview's $19 plan for up to 500 orders a month is sized for that. In Growthify those same four jobs are a quarter of the list; bundles, upsells, loyalty, consent, reports and a page builder come in the same subscription.

Growthify isn't installable yet, because it isn't on the Shopify App Store. The [waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact) is how you hear when it is.

## Four jobs, four places

Wooview's four jobs map onto three Growthify modules and one screen:

| On Wooview | In Growthify |
|---|---|
| Product reviews | [Reviews](/modules/reviews) |
| Wishlist | [Wishlist](/modules/wishlist) |
| Signup and sales popups | [Popups](/modules/popups) |
| Email marketing | [Admin features: marketing and growth](/admin/features#marketing-and-growth) |

The email row is the one that changes how you work. Campaigns in Growthify need your own email provider's key, and Wooview's listing doesn't ask for one. So set up a provider before anything else.

Growthify doesn't send order or shipping confirmations; Shopify keeps sending its own. It has no standalone discount code manager either: codes come only from popup submissions and review incentives. When you're ready, [Installation](/getting-started/installation) covers the rest, for when installing becomes possible.
