# Moving from Yotpo Product Reviews to Growthify

**Last checked November 7, 2026.** Yotpo Product Reviews is the reviews product from Yotpo. It's free up to 50 orders a month; the paid plans change price with order volume and may be billed outside Shopify. <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/yotpo-social-reviews" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Yotpo's listing</a> is our source.

Yotpo's listing puts Google Seller Ratings and shoppable ads up front. Those are the reason to stay if they matter to you and a price that moves with your orders doesn't bother you. Growthify's plan price stays flat as orders grow, and reviews are counted with 15 other modules instead of billed on their own.

Growthify can't be installed today; it isn't on the Shopify App Store yet. You can [join the waitlist](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/contact).

## Settle the ads question before the reviews

Growthify's reviews module does neither of those jobs: no seller ratings, no shoppable ads. If your ads depend on them, a move would cost you that, and nothing below changes it.

If they don't, two things are left to check:

- **The import.** The app has a review import for Yotpo, as API endpoints. It has never run against a live store, because no store has Growthify yet.
- **The meter.** Growthify's plans count reviews. Read [Plans and limits](/admin/billing-plans) and the [Reviews](/modules/reviews) page with your own total in mind.

[Installation](/getting-started/installation) is written for when installing becomes possible.

Growthify won't send order or shipping confirmations; Shopify keeps sending those. There's also no standalone discount code manager. Codes are made for popup submissions and review incentives, and nowhere else.

## Three rows, and why only three

Yotpo's listing stated very little outside reviews. When we read it, nothing was said about popups, wishlists, bundles, cart or post-purchase upsells, loyalty, referrals, email campaigns, abandoned-cart emails, discount codes, order confirmation emails, a cookie banner, a page builder or reports. Those fourteen rows are hidden, which doesn't mean Yotpo lacks them.
