# White-label licence

**A Growthify white-label licence lets you offer Growthify to your own merchants under your own name, while we host it, deploy it and keep it updated.** You don't get a copy of the code and you don't run servers. You get an app with your name on it, on your domain, that your merchants install from you.

This page explains what that means in practice: who it is for, what we run, what each platform group covers, how it differs from buying the source outright, and where it stops. Prices live on the [white-label pricing page](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing), which reads them live from our licensing service. They're not repeated here.

## Who this is for

Growthify replaces a stack of single-purpose Shopify apps with one app of 16 modules. Three kinds of buyer have asked to run that under their own name:

- **An agency that builds Shopify stores.** Every client store ends up with the same five or six apps. A licence lets the agency hand each client one app it controls, with one support thread, instead of six vendors.
- **A company that already sells to Shopify merchants.** A theme shop, a hosting company or a consultancy that wants a conversion suite with its own brand, without building one.
- **A group that runs several stores.** Owning the app across all of them is simpler than renting the same five subscriptions for each store.

If you run one store and want to use Growthify on it, you don't need a licence. Install the app and pick a merchant plan; the [billing plans page](../admin/billing-plans.md) covers those.

## What we run for you

A licensed copy is a full deployment of Growthify that we operate, presented as yours:

- **The embedded Shopify app.** The Remix app that runs inside the Shopify admin, registered as an app in **your** Shopify Partner account under the app name you choose. Your merchants see your name in their admin and on their app list.
- **The theme app extension.** All 72 theme blocks and the single app embed, carrying your name in the theme editor. Blocks are described on the [theme blocks page](../storefront/theme-blocks.md).
- **The Shopify Functions and UI extensions** that ship with the app.
- **The merchant site and dashboard** on your domain, in your colors, with your logo.
- **Hosting, deploys and updates.** We keep the deployment running and ship updates to it. Six months of updates are included from the day you pay; after that you can renew. The [updates page](./updates.md) explains what an update is.

What you bring: your Shopify Partner account, your domain, your brand assets, and the decisions only you can make, such as your app name. The [setup page](./setup.md) walks through the order those happen in.

## The Shopify part, plainly

Your copy is your app. It lives in your Partner account, merchants install it from you, and Shopify bills them through your account under the plans the app ships with.

A listing on the Shopify App Store is not part of the licence. If you want one, you apply for it under your Partner account and Shopify reviews it against its own requirements. We prepare the build and the technical answers the review asks for, but the decision is Shopify's, so we never promise a listing. Without one, your copy is distributed the way Shopify allows apps outside the store to be installed, through your own Partner account.

## Platform groups

Growthify is priced in platform groups. A licence covers the groups you pay for.

| Group | What it covers | Offered today |
|---|---|---|
| Web | The embedded Shopify app, the theme app extension with its 72 blocks, the Shopify Functions and UI extensions, and the merchant site and dashboard | Yes |
| Mobile | The Android app, with iOS delivered once you provide or fund an Apple developer account | No: the Android app isn't on Google Play yet |

Only groups the product actually ships are offered. When the Android app is live on Google Play, the mobile group joins the pricing page, and a two-group licence takes a bundle discount. Until then, the pricing page shows the web group alone and says why.

## Licence or buyout

There are two ways to buy Growthify.

**A licence** is what this section describes. We run it, you sell it. It isn't exclusive: we may license Growthify to other companies too, each under their own name. There's no source code, and six months of updates are included.

**A [source buyout](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout)** hands over the source code of every repository that builds the groups Growthify ships, with six months of updates. After a buyout we stop selling Growthify to anyone else, and the site says so the same day. Licences sold before the buyout keep running on their own terms, so a licensee is never cut off by someone else's purchase. The buyer then pays us a royalty of 7% of the gross revenue Growthify earns, not counting sales tax or VAT collected, every quarter with a revenue statement, for as long as it earns, and still after a resale.

Licences, renewals and buyouts are paid by Wise bank transfer. The payment page shows the transfer details; once we confirm the transfer, the licence key arrives by email.

## Changes made for you

A licence gives you Growthify as it is. If your merchants need something it doesn't do, such as a module they keep asking for, your own plan names and limits, or an integration with a tool your clients already pay for, that's [custom work](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work). It's quoted on its own before work starts and paid separately. Custom changes are not counted as updates, and they don't flow to other licensees.

## What a licence does not include

- **Source code.** Not shown, not shared, not escrowed. Only a buyout transfers it.
- **Exclusivity.** Other companies may license the same product until a buyout.
- **A Shopify App Store listing.** Yours to apply for, Shopify's to approve.
- **Costs you choose.** Your domain, your Partner account, any store accounts and paid services are yours.
- **Updates past the included months** without a renewal. Your copy keeps running on its last version.

## How a licence is checked

Every copy we deploy carries a licence key and serves a small licence file under `/.well-known/` on its own domain. Anyone, including your merchants, can check a key or a domain on the [verify page](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/verify-license). The [verification page](./verify.md) of this section explains the file and what each answer means.

## FAQ

### What is a Growthify white-label licence?

A licence to offer Growthify to your own merchants under your name. We host and deploy it, connect your domain and apply your name, logo and colors. It includes six months of updates and no source code.

### Does a white-label licence include the source code?

No. Source code changes hands only with a source buyout, which also ends sales of Growthify to anyone else.

### Will my white-label copy be listed on the Shopify App Store?

Not as part of the licence. Your copy is an app in your own Shopify Partner account. You can apply for a listing; Shopify reviews it, and no listing is promised.

### Which platform groups can I license today?

The web group: the embedded Shopify app, the theme app extension and the merchant site. The Android app is not on Google Play yet, so the mobile group is not offered.

### Can another company license the same product?

Yes, until somebody buys Growthify outright. After a buyout no new licences are sold, and licences sold before it keep their terms.

### Is there anything to pay after a buyout?

Yes, a royalty. The buyer pays us 7% of the gross revenue Growthify earns, not counting sales tax or VAT collected. It's paid every quarter with a revenue statement, for as long as Growthify earns revenue, and it's still owed after the buyer resells it.

### Where are the prices?

On the [white-label pricing page](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing), which reads them live from the licensing service. This documentation does not repeat them, so it can never disagree with the price you would pay.
