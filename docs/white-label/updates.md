# Updates and renewals

**Every Growthify white-label licence, and every source buyout, includes six months of updates from the day you pay; after that, a renewal of 3, 6 or 12 months extends them.** Without a renewal nothing stops working. Your copy keeps running on the version it has.

This page is for whoever looks after a licensed copy once it's live. It explains what an update is, what isn't one, how the end date is counted, and how to renew. The renewal prices are on the [updates page of the Growthify site](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates), read live from the licensing service, so they aren't copied here.

## What an update is

An update is a change we ship to Growthify itself, deployed to your copy while your updates run:

- **Fixes.** A bug found in any copy of Growthify is fixed in Growthify, and the fix reaches your copy.
- **Improvements** to the modules, the theme blocks and the merchant site that we make for the product as a whole.
- **Changes Shopify makes every app adopt.** Shopify retires Admin API versions on a schedule and changes requirements for embedded apps and theme app extensions. Keeping up with those is part of an update, and it's the part that matters most: an app left on a retired API version eventually stops working in Shopify, whatever else it does.

The [modules overview](../modules/overview.md) lists what's in Growthify today; anything added to that list while your updates run reaches your copy as an update.

## How an update reaches your copy

Growthify is one codebase. A fix or an improvement lands there first, goes through the same checks every release of Growthify goes through, and is then deployed to each copy whose updates are running. You don't install anything and your merchants don't reinstall the app. What they see depends on the part that changed:

- **The merchant site and dashboard** change on the next page load after the deploy.
- **The embedded app** changes the next time a merchant opens it in their Shopify admin.
- **The theme app extension** reaches stores through Shopify's own extension release. A merchant's theme keeps the blocks it has placed; new or changed blocks appear in the theme editor.
- **A change to the app's Shopify scopes** asks each merchant to approve the new access the next time they open the app. That's Shopify's rule, and we tell you before such an update ships.

If an update would change something your merchants rely on, such as a setting moving or a block being replaced, we tell you before it reaches your copy, so you can warn them in your own words.

## What isn't an update

- **Changes made only for your copy.** Your own module, plan structure or integration is [custom work](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted and paid separately. Its upkeep is part of that quote, not of your updates.
- **Branding changes.** A new logo or colors are applied in a deploy, but they're your setup, not a product update.
- **Moving to a new Shopify Partner account**, or anything else that happens in accounts only you control.

## How the end date is counted

Your licence carries an "updates until" date. You can see it at any time on the [verify page](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/verify-license) by entering your key.

- **A new licence** sets it six months after the purchase date.
- **A renewal** adds its months to the later of two dates: the day you renew, or your current "updates until" date. Renewing a month early doesn't cost you that month. Renewing a month late starts the new months from the renewal day, because there's nothing to extend.
- **One renewal per payment**, for one licence key.

For example: your updates run until 30 April. You renew for six months on 10 April. Your new date is 30 October, not 10 October.

## What happens when updates end

Nothing is switched off. Your copy keeps running on its last version, your merchants keep using it, and the licence stays valid. What stops is new deploys of Growthify's changes to your copy.

The risk of letting updates lapse is Shopify's schedule rather than ours. Shopify's API versions are supported for a limited time, and an app that doesn't move forward eventually loses access to a retired one. A renewal is how your copy keeps up.

## Renewing

You renew on the [updates page](https://growthify.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with two things: your licence key, and the email the licence was issued to. Pick 3, 6 or 12 months and continue to payment. The two must match. If they don't, the page says it couldn't match them and nothing else, so nobody can use that page to find out which email a key belongs to.

While online payment isn't switched on for Growthify, the same page shows a request form instead. Send it with your key and the months you want, and we reply by email with the next step.

## After a buyout

A buyout includes six months of updates too. Because you hold the code after a buyout, you can also ship your own changes, but if you want us to keep shipping Growthify's, the same renewal plans apply.

## FAQ

### How many months of updates does a Growthify licence include?

Six months from the day you pay. A source buyout includes six months too.

### What happens when my updates end?

Your copy keeps running on the version it has. Nothing is switched off. A renewal brings updates back.

### How is a renewal counted if I renew early?

From the later of the renewal date and the day your current updates end, so renewing early adds the full months and loses nothing.

### Is a change made only for my copy an update?

No. Updates are what we ship to Growthify itself. Changes for one copy are custom work, quoted separately.

### Do I need my licence key to renew?

Yes, with the email the licence was issued to. The two must match, which stops anyone renewing or probing a key that is not theirs.

### Can a buyout be renewed?

Yes. After a buyout the same renewal plans are available if you want us to keep shipping updates to your copy.
